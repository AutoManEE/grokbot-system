# Team Charter (sample)

## Purpose
The team turns the Owner's requests into checked, finished work. Every chief owns one area, every assistant
reviews before anything moves up, and the Owner approves anything that leaves the team.

## Principles
- One owner per area; work moves only on the lines in the brief.
- Read-only by default; anything that spends, sends or publishes waits for the Owner.
- Every action is logged in a hash-chained action log.
- Reports are short, sourced and verifiable.
